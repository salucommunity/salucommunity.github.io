SSC

Student Society Center

SSC stands for Student Society Center. It is the place where SALU students find their society, build skills and make campus life count.

What is the SSC?

University is more than classes. The Student Society Center brings students together around their interests, so that every student can find a group to learn with, lead in and enjoy campus life with. The SALU Prospectus 2027 lists a Student Society Center among the university's extension services, and the Directorate of Student Affairs encourages and supports students in organising academic events, cultural programs, sports activities and community service.

The center coordinates around 45 active student societies across academic, cultural, literary, social and community-development areas, and student activities are supported by sports facilities, a library and tennis courts.

This page is the SALU Community guide to that side of university life: what student societies do, which events to look forward to and how you can get involved.

What the SSC is for

Find your society

Literary, sports, arts, technology or community service. Meet students who share your interest and learn from seniors.

Events and competitions

Debates, quizzes, exhibitions, sports weeks and cultural programs where you can take part, perform or help organise.

Leadership and teamwork

Planning an event teaches budgeting, communication and leading a team, skills that no exam paper can measure.

Workshops and skills

Public speaking, writing, design, coding and entrepreneurship sessions that add to your degree and your CV.

Community service

Tree plantation, blood donation, help for flood-affected people and seminars on local issues. Societies help students give back.

A voice for students

Ideas and concerns from students reach the right people through representatives and the Directorate of Student Affairs.

How to get involved

  1. Follow the calendar and announcements. Events and notices appear on the Announcements page.
  2. Talk to your department representative. They know which activities are running in your department. You can also join the SALU Community team.
  3. Visit the Directorate of Student Affairs. It supports student activities, counselling and welfare, and is the official office for society matters on campus.
  4. Start small. Volunteer at one event, then take on a role. Most society leaders began exactly that way.
