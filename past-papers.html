How to prepare with SALU past papers

The SALU entry test is a multiple choice test, so repeated practice with past papers is the fastest way to understand the question style. Start with the latest papers, solve each one under the timer, then read the answer key and note the topics where you lost marks.

Use the question bank to search a topic across every paper, and the random mock test to practise a mixed paper. Final admission rules and test dates are decided only by the university, so always check the official notice.

How many SALU entry test past papers are available?

This page has 12 papers: 2015 to 2020, 2022 to 2026, and a 2027 preparation paper, with 555 solved MCQs in total.

Can I download the SALU past papers as PDF?

How does the free online quiz work?

Choose a paper and tap Start Quiz. Answer the MCQs one by one or all together, use the timer and flags, then submit to see your score and the full answer key.
