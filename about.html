About Us

Read the story of SALU Community.

SALU Community is a student-run guidance platform dedicated to supporting applicants and students of Shah Abdul Latif University, Khairpur. For the past three years, our team has helped thousands of students understand admission requirements, check merit lists, calculate aggregates, and prepare for the entry test free of cost.
