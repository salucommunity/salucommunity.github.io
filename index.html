About SALU Community

SALU Community is a student-led support platform for Shah Abdul Latif University, Khairpur. For about three years we have guided new students through admissions, merit lists, departments and entry test preparation, so they no longer need to ask around for basic information.

Everything we share is free and made for student welfare, with no profit motive. We are an independent student community and not an official group of Shah Abdul Latif University Khairpur.

Admissions

A simple walkthrough for new students. Dates and rules change every year, so confirm them on the official Shah Abdul Latif University Khairpur notice.

How admission works
  • SALU announces admissions and the application schedule.
  • You apply and choose your department preferences.
  • You appear in the entry test.
  • SALU publishes merit lists based on your aggregate.
  • Selected students complete the fee and document process.
Documents students usually keep ready
  • Matric and Intermediate mark sheets and certificates
  • CNIC or B-Form
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Domicile, where required
Choosing your department

Compare your aggregate with the closing aggregates on the Departments page, then list departments in order of preference, including a few safer choices.

Admission dates and schedules are posted in the Student Support group. See Contact.

Merit Lists

Merit lists, self-finance merit lists and closing aggregates are shared here as soon as SALU announces them.
