How admission works
  • SALU announces admissions and the application schedule.
  • You apply and choose your department preferences.
  • You appear in the entry test.
  • SALU publishes merit lists based on your aggregate.
  • Selected students complete the fee and document process.
Documents students usually keep ready
  • Matric and Intermediate mark sheets and certificates
  • CNIC or B-Form
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Domicile, where required

Admission Guidance

Plain explanations of the things new students ask most.

How is the aggregate calculated?

For BS Part-I, the entry test counts for 50%, Matric for 20% and Intermediate for 30%. Each part is converted to a percentage of its total marks, then multiplied by its weight.

What if my entry test marks are low?

An entry test score below 17 counts as a fail, so no aggregate is calculated.

How do I read a merit list?

Find your department, then check whether your aggregate is at or above the last selected candidate. That last figure is the closing aggregate.

What is a closing aggregate?

It is the aggregate of the last student admitted to a department in a merit list. It changes every year, so use it as a guide, not a guarantee.

How should I choose departments?

Rank your preferred departments first, then add some with lower closing aggregates as backups.
