About SALU Community

SALU Community is a student-led support platform for Shah Abdul Latif University, Khairpur. For about three years we have guided new students through admissions, merit lists, departments and entry test preparation, so they no longer need to ask around for basic information.

Everything we share is free and made for student welfare, with no profit motive. We are an independent student community and not an official group of Shah Abdul Latif University Khairpur.
