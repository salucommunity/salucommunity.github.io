How to Apply (Step-by-Step)
  1. Create an account on the official SALU online admission portal (www.salu.edu.pk) using your own CNIC or B-Form number.
  2. Fill in your personal and academic details and select your preferred departments in order of priority.
  3. Generate and pay the admission processing fee challan of Rs. 2,500/- (non-refundable) via HBL Konnect.
  4. Upload your paid challan receipt and required documents on the online portal, then print the application form and submit it with attested copies and the original challan to the Directorate of Admissions.
  5. Download your Admit Slip when announced and appear in the Pre-Entry Test on the scheduled date.
Documents Required
  • SSC (Matriculation) Marks Sheet & Pacca Certificate (attested copies).
  • HSC (Intermediate) Part-I / Part-II Marks Sheet & Certificate (passed between 2023 and 2026 for BS Part-I).
  • 2-Year Bachelor / Associate Degree (ADE/ADS/ADC) Marks Sheet & Pass Certificate (passed in 2021 or onwards with at least 50% marks for BS Part-III Bridging).
  • Candidate's CNIC or B-Form copy and Father/Guardian's CNIC copy.
  • Domicile & PRC (Form-D) of the candidate or Father/Mother.
  • Three recent passport-size photographs attested on the back.
  • Original Transfer/Leaving Certificate (TC), Character Certificate, and Undertaking on Rs. 50/- Stamp Paper at the time of enrollment.
  • Eligibility Certificate (required only if passing Intermediate/Bachelor from a board/university other than BISE Sukkur or BISE Larkana).
General Eligibility Criteria
  • BS Part-I (4-Year) & Pharm-D (5-Year): Intermediate (HSC-II) or equivalent passed between 2023 and 2026. Pharm-D, Biochemistry, Botany, Microbiology, and Zoology require Pre-Medical; BS Mathematics requires Pre-Engineering; Chemistry, Physics, Statistics, and Archaeology accept Pre-Medical or Pre-Engineering; Computer Science, IT, and Software Engineering accept Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, or DAE; all other departments accept Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, Commerce, Arts, and DAE.
  • LLB (4-Year): Intermediate in any group plus passing the HEC Law Admission Test (LAT).
  • BS Part-III (Bridging — 2 Years / 4 Semesters): 2-Year Bachelor (B.A./B.Sc./B.Com.) or Associate Degree (ADE/ADS/ADC) passed in 2021 or onwards with at least 50% marks.
  • Sub-Campuses (Ghotki & Shahdad Kot): Offer 4-Year BS programs in Business Administration (BBA), Computer Science & I.T., and English Language & Literature.
Hostels & Campus Facilities

Separate hostel accommodation is available on the Main Campus for male and female students (5 Boys Hostels and 3 Girls Hostels) at an annual hostel fee of Rs. 14,000/-. Rooms are allotted to regular enrolled students on a distance/district basis. Campus facilities also include the Central Library & Book Bank, Smart WiFi, Sports Complex, Medical Dispensary, and University Bus Transport.

TEST PATTERN

Entry Test Preparation

Understand the SALU Pre-Entry Test structure, MCQ distribution, and test-day instructions.

50 MCQs PAPER PATTERN

Paper Structure & Syllabus

  • Total Questions: 50 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) with 4 options (A, B, C, D).
  • Section A — 30 Questions (General & Languages): English, Sindhi, Urdu, General Knowledge, Current Affairs, Pakistan Studies, and Islamic Studies.
  • Section B — 20 Questions (Subject Group): Intermediate-level questions from your selected group (Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, Commerce, or Arts).
  • Merit Weightage: The Entry Test carries 50% weightage in the final merit calculation.
TEST-DAY INSTRUCTIONS

Important OMR Sheet Rules

  • Bring your printed Admit Slip, original CNIC/B-Form, and a 7000 # 2 Goldfish pencil.
  • Fill the chosen circle completely; double-marked or half-filled circles are marked wrong.
  • Calculators, mobile phones, smartwatches, and electronic devices are not allowed in the test center.
  • Do not fold or bend the computer OMR answer sheet.

About SALU Community

Who we are and how we support students of Shah Abdul Latif University, Khairpur.

SALU Community is a student-run informational and educational platform dedicated to helping prospective and current students of Shah Abdul Latif University, Khairpur. For the past three years, our team has worked consistently to guide students through every stage of the admission and entry test process.

Every year, thousands of students apply to SALU, and many face confusion regarding admission procedures, entry test preparation, merit lists, department selection, and fee structures. SALU Community bridges this information gap by providing clear, reliable, and timely student updates in one place.
